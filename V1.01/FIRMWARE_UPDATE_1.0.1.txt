## Version 1.0.1

### Fixed
- Fixed a deep-sleep wake issue that could prevent the mouse from functioning correctly after reconnecting over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).
- Improved System OFF wake handling by correctly restoring button and input states after waking from deep sleep.
- Improved overall wake-up reliability and BLE reconnection stability.

### Added
- Added a three-button **Factory Reset** feature.
- Factory Reset clears:
  - BLE bonding information
  - LittleFS / InternalFS
  - Saved settings
  - Usage statistics
  - Battery statistics
- The mouse automatically reboots after the reset is complete.

### Notes
- Firmware, bootloader and SoftDevice are preserved during a Factory Reset.
- After performing a Factory Reset, remove the mouse from the host's Bluetooth device list and pair it again.
